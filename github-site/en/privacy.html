Ingtalk Privacy Policy

Effective: August 24, 2026

Itembus (the “Operator”) publishes this Privacy Policy to explain how personal data is handled securely in the Ingtalk service.

1. Data and Purposes

CategoryDataPurpose
Required profileAnonymous user ID, nickname, age or birth year, gender, language, and countryCreate profiles, provide conversation-partner information, and enforce age restrictions

Ingtalk does not currently request contacts or microphone access. Users can reset or delete the advertising ID in device settings. Restricting advertising processing may limit ads or ad-reward features.

2. Retention

  • Active-account profiles and general service data: until account deletion
  • User-accessible chat messages: up to 30 days
  • Closed support inquiries and error logs: up to 90 days
  • Safety message backups and report or enforcement evidence: up to one year
  • Device-wallet and repeat-registration prevention records: up to one year after last use or withdrawal
  • Records required by law: for the legally required period

Expired data is irreversibly deleted. Records needed for a legal obligation, investigation, or dispute may be isolated with restricted access until the required period ends. Anonymous board aliases remain linked to internal identifiers where necessary for safety and legal duties.

3. Service Providers and International Processing

The Operator does not sell personal data or disclose it to third parties except with consent or as required by law. The following providers process data on the Operator's behalf.

International transfers occur over encrypted networks when a feature is used or an error occurs. Data is retained for the period above or until the processing agreement ends. Google's privacy practices are available in the Google Privacy Policy. Users may request restriction or deletion through Support, although this may limit related features.

4. Location and Optional Permissions

Approximate or precise location is used to calculate and display distance ranges. Exact coordinates are not shown to other users. Users can deny location permission and continue using non-location features. Notifications announce chat requests and messages. Only photos explicitly selected through the system picker are processed.

5. User Rights

Users may edit profiles and withdraw permissions under My Info. Accounts and associated data can be deleted under My Info → Delete account. Users can also request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or withdrawal through Account & Data Deletion, itembus@itembus.com, or 1555-1645.

6. Minors

Ingtalk is only for users age 19 or older. Accounts and content may be restricted or deleted if use by a minor is identified.

7. Safeguards and Disposal

The Operator applies encryption in transit, database row-level security, separation and auditing of administrative access, reporting, blocking, and account enforcement. Electronic files are deleted in a manner intended to prevent recovery, while legally retained records are kept separately.
