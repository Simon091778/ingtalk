Community Guidelines

Ingtalk is a space for respectful conversations between users age 19 or older.

Immediate restrictions

  • Child sexual abuse material or sexual approaches to minors
  • Prostitution, compensated dating, illegal recordings, or non-consensual intimate images
  • Specific threats of physical harm, stalking, or disclosure of personal data
  • Fraud, account theft, or repeated requests for money

Content removal or enforcement

  • Unwanted sexual language or persistent pressure to meet
  • Insults, hate, harassment, or repeated unwanted contact
  • Misuse of another person's photo or identity
  • Advertising, spam, or attempts to harvest external contact details
  • Abuse of points or rewards

User safety tools

  • Report posts, comments, aliases, users, or chats
  • Block another user
  • Delete chat rooms
  • Request account and data deletion

Only authorized operators review reports. A reporter's identity is not disclosed to the reported user. Measures may include content removal, warnings, temporary suspension, permanent suspension, or cooperation with authorities.

Immediate danger?

Contact local emergency services instead of waiting for an in-app report.

Submit an enforcement appeal through Support.
